## Support on WhatsApp

When you write to Automagik on WhatsApp, Automagik's support assistant answers, with people from our
team watching and joining in. It only answers you; it never writes first.

Your conversation is isolated in its own sandbox: the assistant uses only your conversation to answer
you. We keep it so the assistant remembers you; nothing is deleted automatically. Your messages reach
us through Omni, our messaging service, and are processed by the AI model behind the assistant. We do
not sell your data, use it for advertising or send you marketing messages.

You can download your conversation or reset it at any time: just ask the assistant, or write to
privacy@automagik.si. A waitlist code you send is recorded as confirmed; the waitlist never stores the
text of your message.
