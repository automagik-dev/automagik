On your phone

Keep your agents close.

See which agents need you, open their terminals and answer from your phone. Teams and code spaces come from your own machines.

Pair with Pocket Link.

  1. Add Pocket Link to Herdr on your computer.
  2. Choose Pair a phone.
  3. Scan the QR code in Automagik Mobile.

No Cloud account is needed.

Set up Pocket Link ↗

Open the APK on your phone and allow your browser to install apps when Android asks. Pocket Link needs Herdr desktop and herdr-core on macOS or Linux. Read the Mobile guide ↗
