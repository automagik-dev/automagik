Privacy, consent and removing your details

Namastex Labs is the controller. Contact privacy@automagik.si for access or correction.

We keep your answers, chosen platform, language, contact channel, consent version and registration time. Email confirmation stores your email and confirmation time. WhatsApp confirmation stores the sender ID or number WhatsApp gives us, the message identifier, confirmation reply, event time and confirmation time, never your message text or sender name. WhatsApp may give us an opaque sender ID instead of a phone number. Confirmation tokens and codes are stored as hashes.

Your consent lets us record and review your interest in Cloud. This is not a Cloud account, a purchase, or consent to training. Only the owner can read or export the list. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

Cloudflare processes the page and stores the list in a database restricted to the European Union. This involves international transfers. Email confirmation uses Resend and its delivery providers; choosing WhatsApp involves WhatsApp/Meta and our Omni. Use email if you prefer not to share your WhatsApp number.

Unconfirmed email entries expire after a day. Send a WhatsApp code within half an hour; confirmation may be processed up to 180 days after code expiry, using the time your message was received. Pending WhatsApp entries are kept for that backlog window. Entries outside their applicable window are deleted on the next request. Confirmed entries stay until Cloud launches or you remove them; the owner reviews retention every six months. Short-lived keyed abuse counters and email cooldown hashes expire after a day. Raw IPs and user agents are not stored by the function.

You can request confirmation, access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent, and complain to the ANPD or your EU supervisory authority. Removing your details withdraws consent. There are no cookies or analytics in this page.

The response is the same whether or not the contact was registered.

Support on WhatsApp

When you write to Automagik on WhatsApp, Automagik's support assistant answers, with people from our team watching and joining in. It only answers you; it never writes first.

Your conversation is isolated in its own sandbox: the assistant uses only your conversation to answer you. We keep it so the assistant remembers you; nothing is deleted automatically. Your messages reach us through Omni, our messaging service, and are processed by the AI model behind the assistant. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or send you marketing messages.

You can download your conversation or reset it at any time: just ask the assistant, or write to privacy@automagik.si. A waitlist code you send is recorded as confirmed; the waitlist never stores the text of your message.

Suporte pelo WhatsApp

Quando você escreve para a Automagik pelo WhatsApp, o assistente de suporte da Automagik responde, com pessoas da nossa equipe acompanhando e participando. Ele só responde a você; nunca inicia a conversa.

Sua conversa fica isolada em seu próprio sandbox: o assistente usa apenas a sua conversa para responder a você. Nós a mantemos para que o assistente se lembre de você; nada é excluído automaticamente. Suas mensagens chegam até nós pelo Omni, nosso serviço de mensagens, e são processadas pelo modelo de IA por trás do assistente. Não vendemos seus dados, não os usamos para publicidade e não enviamos mensagens de marketing.

Você pode baixar sua conversa ou reiniciá-la a qualquer momento: basta pedir ao assistente ou escrever para privacy@automagik.si. Um código da lista de espera que você enviar é registrado como confirmado; a lista de espera nunca armazena o texto da sua mensagem.
