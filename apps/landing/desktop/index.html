Make it yours

Your model. Your files. Your team.

Bring your AI.

Connect an AI account or an API key. Each Bot runs on the model you give it. An Omni and an AI account are things to connect when you are ready; both can wait.

Keep your files and memory.

The team keeps its files in your computer’s .automagik folder. What Bots read and write goes to your connected AI provider under its terms.

Secrets the Bots never see.

Type keys and passwords into a card in the chat. The Bot never sees the value.

Permission, with clear limits.

It asks before sending as you to a person, deleting something or disconnecting a channel. Main and Omni Bots can read chats and post to groups, WhatsApp channels and your own chat without asking. Other Bots ask for chat access; an Always grant lets them post to that group again.

Install the app.

The app’s switch still says Work for Team mode. Code mode installs separately.

Mac

  1. Open Automagik-Mac.dmg.
  2. Drag the Automagik icon to Applications.
  3. Open Automagik from Applications.

Linux

  1. Open Automagik-Linux.deb and choose Install, or run sudo apt install ./Automagik-Linux.deb.
  2. Open Automagik from your apps.

On another Linux, use the AppImage.
